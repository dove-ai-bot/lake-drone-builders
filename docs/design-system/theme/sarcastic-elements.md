# Sarcastic Design Elements

## Overview
Industrial humor through over-engineered seriousness. Every mundane product gets the treatment of critical infrastructure components, creating comedy through absurd technical gravitas.

## Implementation Guidelines

### Badge Placement Strategy
- Primary ISO certifications: Product cards, prominently displayed
- Military standards: Technical specifications sections
- Security warnings: Checkout process, shipping information
- Performance badges: Product detail pages
- Compliance statements: Footer, legal sections

### Tone Consistency
- Always completely serious in presentation
- Use authentic formatting and language
- Reference real standards and regulations
- Make claims technically absurd but linguistically proper
- Maintain deadpan delivery throughout

### User Experience Considerations
- Don't overwhelm the interface with too many badges
- Use progressive disclosure for detailed warnings
- Ensure humor enhances rather than impedes shopping
- Maintain clear product information hierarchy
- Keep actual product details easily accessible

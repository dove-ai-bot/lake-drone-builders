### Industrial Design Requirements
[Source: Architecture research on design system]
**Typography**:
- Product names: `font-industrial` (uppercase, bold, wide tracking)
- Part numbers: `font-mono-tech` (JetBrains Mono)
- Specifications: `font-mono` with reduced size
- Prices: Bold with "UNIT COST:" prefix

**Color Usage**:
- Card background: Gunmetal gradient (steel-800 to steel-900)
- Primary CTA: Safety orange (#ff4000)
- Hover state: Brighten by 10% with shadow-lg
- Disabled/Requisitioned: 50% opacity with stamp overlay
- Text: Light gray (zinc-200) on dark backgrounds

**Layout Specifications**:
- Card padding: 1.5rem (24px)
- Grid gap: 1.5rem on desktop, 1rem on mobile
- Min card height: Ensure consistency across grid
- Image aspect ratio: Maintain 4:3 or square format

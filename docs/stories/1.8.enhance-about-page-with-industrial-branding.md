### Industrial Design Requirements
[Source: docs/design-system/theme/industrial-design-system.md]
**Design Philosophy**: Deadpan Industrial Seriousness - Every element presented with gravitas of critical infrastructure components

**Color Palette Guidelines**:
- Steel grays (#1a1d21 to #f5f6f7): Primary interface, text, backgrounds
- Safety orange (#ff4000): Primary CTAs, important actions, alerts  
- Caution yellow (#ffb400): Warnings, badges, highlights
- Tech blue (#007fff): Links, secondary actions, technical indicators

**Typography System**:
- Inter: Primary UI text and headings
- JetBrains Mono: Technical specifications, codes, part numbers
- Roboto Condensed: Large display headings only

**Visual Effects**:
- Industrial shadows with rivet and panel effects
- Metal gradients for depth and texture
- Technical grid patterns and borders
- Mechanical animation timing and easing

### Component Architecture
[Source: src/app/components/industrial/core/ analysis]
**Available Industrial Components**:
- `IndustrialButton`: Metallic styling with mechanical animations, variants: primary, secondary, technical, warning
- `IndustrialCard`: Blueprint-style containers with rivet corners, variants: standard, technical, specification, warning, blueprint
- `IndustrialBadge`: Certification stamps and indicators, types: certification, warning, status, classification
- `IndustrialInput`: Technical form elements for future contact forms

**Component Props and Variants**:
- Size options: sm, md, lg, xl
- Classification levels: unclassified, confidential, secret, top-secret
- Badge variants: iso, mil-spec, safety, tech, compliance
- Card variants: standard, technical, specification, warning, blueprint
